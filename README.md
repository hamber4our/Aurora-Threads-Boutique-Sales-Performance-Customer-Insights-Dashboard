# Aurora-Threads-Boutique-Sales-Performance-Customer-Insights-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Aurora Threads Boutique’s sales performance across three stores. Explores revenue trends, top-performing products, customer purchasing behaviour, and payment preferences to uncover actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.
# CASE STUDY: AURORA THREADS BOUTIQUE
## Sales Performance, Customer Behaviour & Product Analysis Using Power BI

## 1. Business Introduction

Aurora Threads Boutique is a high-end fashion retailer specializing in designer clothing, footwear, and accessories. Known for its distinctive collections and personalized shopping experience, the boutique serves fashion-conscious customers across multiple store locations.

Operating in a competitive retail environment requires a strong understanding of customer preferences, product performance, and sales trends. To remain competitive and identify opportunities for growth, Aurora Threads Boutique needs to leverage data-driven strategies to improve business performance and decision-making.

## 2. Problem Statement

As Aurora Threads Boutique expands its operations and accumulates more sales data, extracting meaningful insights becomes increasingly important.

Without a centralized analytical solution, the business faces several challenges:

- Difficulty identifying top-performing products and product categories.
- Limited visibility into customer purchasing behaviour and revenue contributions.
- Challenges in monitoring sales performance across different store locations.
- Limited understanding of preferred payment methods and purchasing patterns.
- Difficulty tracking daily sales fluctuations and identifying opportunities for revenue growth.

These challenges can make it difficult for management to make timely, informed decisions regarding inventory, marketing, customer engagement, and overall business performance.

## 3. Aim and Objectives of the Project

The primary aim of this project is to develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that transforms Aurora Threads Boutique's sales data into actionable business insights.

The specific objectives are to:

- Monitor key performance indicators, including total revenue, quantity sold, number of stores, and total customers.
- Identify the highest-revenue product categories.
- Determine the top 10 products contributing to total revenue.
- Analyze sales trends over time to identify fluctuations and peak-performing days.
- Examine revenue distribution across customer gender segments.
- Evaluate customer payment preferences.
- Analyze customer-level revenue and purchase quantities.
- Provide an interactive platform for exploring sales performance across three store locations.

## 4. Dataset and Data Dictionary

The analysis uses retail transaction data containing information about sales transactions, products, customers, payment methods, and store locations.

The key dataset fields include:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Date | Date of each sales transaction |
| Store | Store location associated with the transaction |
| Product | Name or description of the product sold |
| Category | Product category, such as clothing, footwear, or accessories |
| Gender | Target customer segment associated with the product |
| Quantity | Number of units sold |
| Sales Amount | Revenue generated by the transaction |
| Customer ID | Unique identifier used to analyze customer purchasing behaviour |
| Discount | Discount applied to the transaction, if any |
| Payment Type | Payment method used, such as cash, credit card, debit card, or online payment |

These fields provide the foundation for analyzing sales performance, customer segmentation, product contributions, and purchasing patterns.

## 5. Tools and Methodology

**Tools Used**
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data visualization and business intelligence techniques

**Methodology**

**Data Preparation and Transformation**

The dataset was prepared for analysis by organizing relevant transaction fields and ensuring that the data could support meaningful comparisons across products, customers, payment methods, and stores.

**Data Analysis and Calculations**

Key performance indicators and revenue calculations were used to measure business performance. Product, customer, and payment-related metrics were incorporated into the analytical model.

**Dashboard Development**

An interactive Power BI dashboard was designed to consolidate key business metrics and visualizations into a single interface.

The dashboard includes:
- KPI cards for total quantity, total revenue, total stores, and total customers.
- Horizontal bar charts for revenue by product category.
- Product-level revenue comparisons.
- A pie chart showing revenue distribution by gender.
- A pie chart displaying revenue by payment type.
- A customer-level table showing revenue and quantity sold.
- A daily revenue trend chart.
- Interactive filters for Store A, Store B, and Store C.

The dashboard provides an overview of Aurora Threads Boutique's sales performance.

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|

| Total Quantity Sold | 257 |
| Total Stores | 3 |

These indicators provide a high-level snapshot of the business and serve as a starting point for more detailed analysis.

## 7. Key Findings and Business Insights

**7.1 Revenue by Product Category**

Accessories generated the highest revenue, contributing approximately $50K. Clothing followed with $24K, while footwear generated $23K.

This indicates that accessories are a major revenue contributor within the analyzed sales data.

**Business implication:** Management could prioritize the availability and merchandising of high-performing accessories while investigating opportunities to improve clothing and footwear sales.

**7.2 Revenue by Product**

The product-level analysis highlights the top revenue-generating products.

Handbags recorded the highest displayed revenue at approximately $8K, followed by necklaces and sunglasses at around $7.2K each.

Other notable products include boots, loafers, sandals, rings, wristwatches, and belts.

**Business implication:** Product-level revenue comparisons can help management identify high-performing items, make more informed inventory decisions, and develop targeted promotional strategies.

**7.3 Revenue by Gender**

The dashboard reveals the following revenue distribution:

| Customer Segment | Revenue | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unisex | $41K | 42.08% |
| Female | $34K | 35.22% |
| Male | $22K | 22.70% |

Unisex products represent the largest share of the displayed revenue distribution, followed by female and male segments.

**Business implication:** Aurora Threads Boutique could explore targeted marketing strategies for each customer segment while continuing to support the strong performance of its unisex product range.

**7.4 Revenue by Payment Type**

The payment analysis shows the following distribution:

| Payment Method | Revenue | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Online Payment | $30K | 30.38% |
| Cash | $30K | 30.27% |
| Credit Card | $25K | 25.12% |
| Debit Card | $14K | 14.23% |

Online payments and cash represent the largest shares of revenue, with credit card payments also making a substantial contribution.

**Business implication:** Maintaining convenient payment options across different customer preferences could support a smooth purchasing experience and help the boutique retain customers.

**7.5 Daily Revenue Trends**

The daily revenue chart highlights fluctuations in sales throughout the reporting period.

Several days recorded noticeable revenue peaks, while other days experienced lower sales. This variation creates opportunities to investigate the factors influencing daily performance.

**Business implication:** Management can use daily revenue trends to monitor sales patterns, evaluate promotional activities, and improve inventory and staffing decisions.

**7.6 Customer-Level Revenue and Quantity Analysis**

The customer-level table provides a breakdown of individual customer IDs, revenue generated, and quantities purchased.

This allows management to examine differences in customer spending and purchasing volumes.

**Business implication:** Customer-level analysis can help identify high-value customers, understand purchasing patterns, and inform customer retention and loyalty initiatives.

## 8. Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. **Strengthen accessory sales strategies:** Prioritize high-performing accessories through effective merchandising and inventory planning while exploring ways to increase sales in other product categories.

2. **Optimize product inventory:** Use product-level revenue analysis to identify popular products and support better stock allocation and replenishment decisions.

3. **Develop targeted marketing campaigns:** Tailor promotional strategies to different customer segments, paying particular attention to the strong revenue contribution of unisex and female product segments.

4. **Maintain flexible payment options:** Continue supporting online, cash, and card payments to accommodate different purchasing preferences.

5. **Monitor daily sales performance:** Investigate revenue peaks and slower sales periods to identify opportunities for improved promotions, inventory planning, and operational efficiency.

6. **Improve customer retention:** Use customer-level purchasing information to identify valuable customers and explore loyalty programs, personalized offers, and repeat-purchase strategies.

7. **Compare store performance:** Leverage the dashboard's store filters to investigate differences in revenue and sales volume between locations and identify opportunities to improve performance.

## 9. Project Limitations

Although the dashboard provides valuable insights, some limitations should be considered:

- The analysis reflects the transactions available in the supplied dataset and may not represent the boutique's entire business performance.
- The dashboard provides descriptive insights and does not independently establish the causes of sales fluctuations.
- Customer IDs support purchasing analysis, but additional customer information would be required for deeper demographic and behavioural segmentation.
- Further analysis across longer reporting periods would help identify seasonal patterns and longer-term business trends.
- The store filters enable location-level exploration, but detailed comparative conclusions require examining each store's individual results.

## 10. Conclusion

The Aurora Threads Boutique Sales Performance Dashboard demonstrates how business intelligence and data visualization can transform retail transaction data into meaningful business insights.

By analyzing revenue by product category, individual product performance, customer segments, payment preferences, customer-level purchases, and daily sales trends, the project provides management with a clearer understanding of key business performance indicators.

The interactive dashboard also offers the flexibility to explore results across three store locations, supporting more focused business analysis.

Ultimately, this project demonstrates the value of **Power BI, data modelling, DAX, and analytical thinking in solving practical business problems and supporting data-driven decision-making.**
